Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Orbit ("Orbit," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our application (the "Service"), including when you sign in with your Google Account.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in to Orbit using Google Sign-In, we request access to the following information through Google's OAuth service:

  • Email address — used to identify your account and communicate with you.
  • Google Classroom data — used to access and display your relevant classroom information (such as courses, coursework, or rosters, depending on the specific Classroom scopes your account grants) so that Orbit can provide its core features.

We only request the minimum scopes necessary for Orbit to function, and Google will show you exactly what is being requested on the consent screen before you approve access.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your account on Orbit.
  • To retrieve and display your Google Classroom data within the Service.
  • To provide, maintain, and improve the features of Orbit.
  • To communicate with you about your account or the Service, when necessary.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use data obtained through Google APIs for advertising purposes.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

Orbit's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your Google account data with third parties, except:

  • With service providers who help us operate Orbit (e.g. hosting or infrastructure providers), under obligations to protect your data.
  • If required to do so by law, regulation, or valid legal process.
  • With your explicit consent.

5. Data Retention

We retain the information we collect for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 7).

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices & Rights

  • You can revoke Orbit's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account permissions page.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us (see Section 9).

8. Children's Privacy

If Orbit is used in an educational context involving students under 18, data is handled solely to support the core educational functionality provided through Google Classroom, in accordance with applicable school agreements and laws (such as FERPA/COPPA where relevant).
